WHY IT READS FROM FAR AWAY

Distance is won by letter height and contrast, not by brightness alone. LookAt sizes every typeface by cap height, so the letters fill the same share of the screen whichever face you pick, and it measures the real WCAG contrast between your two colours — it warns you before you walk into a dark room with a combination nobody can read, and adds a halo when the pair is genuinely marginal.

SEVEN FACES, ALL CHOSEN FOR DISTANCE

  • Expanded — the widest strokes, and the best raw legibility
  • Rounded, Condensed, Mono, Serif and Slanted, each for a different voice
  • Dot Matrix — a stadium departure board, drawn lamp by lamp

MOVE IT, OR HOLD IT STILL

  • Scroll long messages left or right, at the speed you choose
  • Fixed for the short ones
  • Blink, up to six times a second
  • Mirror mode, for a sign read through glass

HONEST BY DESIGN

No account. No ads. No tracking. No network at all — LookAt has never asked for a connection, so nothing you type ever leaves your phone. Free, with nothing to buy inside.

A note on what a phone can do: a screen is bright, but it is not a stadium display. In direct sunlight, or from more than a few dozen metres, even the best pair of colours loses. LookAt is at its best indoors, after dark and inside the crowd — arrivals halls, concerts, finish lines, school shows.
